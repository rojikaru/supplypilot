# 2 · Product / Research Lead (no-code)

**Mission:** own *what we build and why it wins*. You said you're least comfortable with code — good,
you own everything that isn't code: research, scope, the numbers, and the story. This role is the
difference between a nice demo and a winning one at a Silpo-judged, ROI-loving hackathon.

**Owns:** business interviews, use-case validation, backlog grooming, ROI/impact model, judging-criteria
alignment, demo script, pitch narrative (with #7).
**Interfaces with:** mentor (pull numbers/interviews), Designer (use cases → flows),
Marketing (#7, pitch), Tech Lead (scope cuts).
